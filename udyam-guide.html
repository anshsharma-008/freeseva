Udyam registration: organise your business details

Prepare consistent enterprise information and avoid confusing registration with other obligations.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

Udyam is the official MSME registration route. It does not replace GST obligations, income-tax filing or an activity-specific licence. Read the official eligibility and classification guidance to understand how it applies to your enterprise before beginning.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Confirm your enterprise’s ownership structure and business activity.
  2. Prepare the identifiers required for that ownership type using the portal’s current checklist.
  3. Check the consistency of business name, address and contact information.
  4. Read the official fee notice before using an intermediary that charges for assistance.

Complete the next steps

Use the government Udyam portal, read the declarations and enter information that matches your records. Review activity and enterprise details before submitting. Save the resulting registration details securely and use the official facilities when you need to verify or update them.

Open Udyam Registration ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://udyamregistration.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If you already have a registration, look for the existing-record or update guidance before starting again. Registration does not itself promise a loan, subsidy or tender award. Check each separate programme’s requirements and consult a qualified adviser for obligations that depend on your business circumstances.
