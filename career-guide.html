BENEFITS & CAREERS / GUIDE 16

Use National Career Service with a safer job-search routine

Prepare a useful profile and verify recruiters before sharing documents.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

A job listing is a starting point for research, not a guarantee of employment. Use National Career Service’s official guidance and check the employer and role independently before sharing documents, attending an interview or accepting an offer.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Prepare a current CV with accurate skills, qualifications and experience.
  2. Decide which locations, job types and working arrangements you can consider.
  3. Keep public profile details limited to what a recruiter needs at the early stage.
  4. Read the portal’s fraud warnings and service-charge guidance.

Complete the next steps

Create or update your profile through the official portal, search for suitable roles and read the complete listing. Keep a private record of the employer, vacancy reference, application date and next step. Verify interview messages using independently obtained employer contact details where possible.

If something goes wrong

Pause if a recruiter asks for an advance payment, a bank OTP or remote access to your phone. Do not assume a familiar logo proves authenticity. Report suspicious listings through the relevant portal channel. Digital Seva Hub does not recruit, conduct interviews or guarantee placement.
