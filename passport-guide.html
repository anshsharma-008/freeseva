Passport application and appointment preparation

Keep the application, appointment and document-check stages organised.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

Passport requirements depend on the type of application and the applicant’s circumstances. First distinguish a fresh application from a reissue and consult the official document guidance. An old checklist shared by a friend may not cover your case.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Select the right application category and read the corresponding document instructions.
  2. Check names, date of birth and address details against supporting records.
  3. Read appointment, payment and rescheduling rules before choosing a slot.
  4. Keep the required originals and supporting copies ready for the appointment.

Complete the next steps

Complete the form through Passport Seva, review your particulars carefully and follow the payment and appointment steps shown there. Save the application reference, payment receipt and appointment confirmation as separate records. Check the latest visit instructions before leaving for the centre.

Open Passport Seva ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://www.passportindia.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If money is debited but no appointment is visible, check the official payment-status and booking guidance before paying again. A booked appointment does not mean a passport has been approved. Track the application through Passport Seva and use its support routes for verification or processing questions.
