BENEFITS & CAREERS / GUIDE 15

e-Shram registration and profile maintenance

Check registration requirements and keep worker details accurate.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

e-Shram provides a registration route for eligible unorganised workers. Read the official eligibility guidance before beginning. Registration should not be treated as an automatic entitlement to every welfare benefit mentioned in a forwarded message.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Check the current eligibility rules for your employment situation.
  2. Prepare the identity and contact information required by the official registration route.
  3. Choose occupation and address details that reflect your circumstances.
  4. Check the official help options if you cannot complete the authentication steps.

Complete the next steps

Open e-Shram, follow the registration or existing-profile route and review the data before submitting. Save the registration information privately. When details change, use the portal’s official update guidance rather than creating a second record without checking whether one already exists.

Open e-Shram ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://eshram.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If you are seeking a specific benefit, read that programme’s separate conditions and application steps. A registration card alone does not prove that a payment has been sanctioned. For authentication, record corrections or service-centre assistance, use contact details published by e-Shram and keep OTPs private.
