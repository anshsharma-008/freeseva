Find the right department through UMANG

Use a shared service gateway without losing track of the department handling your request.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

UMANG brings services from multiple departments into a common gateway. Availability, eligibility and processing still depend on the relevant department. Begin with the task you need to complete, rather than assuming all services listed in the app work the same way.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Note the department, scheme or service name you want to find.
  2. Check whether the service covers your state or location.
  3. Read whether it provides information, status tracking or a complete application.
  4. Prepare only the identifiers and documents requested by that particular service.

Complete the next steps

Open UMANG’s official site or app and locate the department. Read the service description before signing in or supplying information. Save any reference generated by the service and note which department owns it so you can return to the correct support channel.

Open UMANG ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://web.umang.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If a service is unavailable, check the department’s own site for an alternative route. An UMANG account does not automatically enrol you in every linked scheme. For a pending benefit or application, quote the departmental reference to the responsible helpdesk rather than treating a gateway login as proof of submission.
