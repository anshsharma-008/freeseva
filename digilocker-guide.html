IDENTITY & DOCUMENTS / GUIDE 03

Find and share documents with DigiLocker

Know when to fetch an issued document and when an uploaded scan is useful.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

DigiLocker can provide documents made available by participating issuers. A file you upload yourself is different from a record fetched from an issuer. Before relying on either, ask the receiving institution which format and document it accepts for your specific task.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Identify the issuing organisation and the exact document you need.
  2. Keep the issuer’s reference or identifying details available privately.
  3. Check that the document belongs to you and that the displayed particulars are accurate.
  4. Read the receiving portal’s requirements before downloading or sharing a copy.

Complete the next steps

Sign in through DigiLocker’s official site or app, find the issuer and follow the document-fetch instructions. Review the returned record rather than assuming a successful search means every field is correct. Share through an appropriate supported method and avoid leaving copies on shared devices.

Open DigiLocker ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://www.digilocker.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If no document appears, check the issuer, reference details and issuer participation. A missing result does not itself invalidate a physical document. For an error in the source record, use the issuing organisation’s correction channel; editing a downloaded file does not correct that record.
