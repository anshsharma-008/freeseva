PAN applications, corrections and reprints

Choose the correct PAN task before submitting a new application.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

A new PAN application, a correction to existing details and a replacement card are different requests. If you already have a PAN, start with the correction or reprint guidance instead of applying for another number. The official service instructions explain the available application routes and verification methods.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Identify whether you need a new PAN, a correction or a reprint.
  2. Read the identity, address and date-of-birth evidence requirements for your applicant category.
  3. Check the spelling and order of names before filling in the form.
  4. Review the delivery address and the selected physical or electronic delivery option.

Complete the next steps

Follow the application route linked by the provider, review the completed form and check the displayed charges before paying. Some routes require additional signing, verification or document submission. Save the acknowledgement and follow its instructions until the application is complete.

If something goes wrong

A successful payment alone does not confirm that all verification steps are finished. If the application stalls, use its acknowledgement to check status and contact the provider. Avoid sending full PAN details in a public complaint or posting a card image on social media.
