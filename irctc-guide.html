TRAVEL & TRANSPORT / GUIDE 08

Rail booking: from search to journey checks

Read ticket status, passenger details and refund rules before booking.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

A train search result is not a reservation. Availability can change while you enter details, and a booking may have a status that requires further attention. Choose your travel date, stations and class carefully, then read the current booking conditions before authorising payment.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Confirm station codes, travel date and boarding point rather than relying on similar city names.
  2. Enter passenger particulars as required and check the identification rules.
  3. Review class, quota, fare and the displayed availability or waitlist status.
  4. Read cancellation and refund conditions for the ticket type you are buying.

Complete the next steps

Book only through the official provider or an authorised route you have verified. After payment, check booking history and save the ticket and PNR privately. Before travelling, review the current reservation status and train information through the railway’s official services.

Open IRCTC ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://www.irctc.co.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If the account shows a debit without a ticket, check transaction and booking history before trying again. Do not assume a payment screenshot is valid travel authority. Refund timing and eligibility depend on the transaction and railway rules; use IRCTC’s support process with the relevant reference.
