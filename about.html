About Digital Seva Hub

An independent starting point for everyday services.

What this site does

Digital Seva Hub organises links to Indian public-service portals and publishes practical guidance for people preparing to use them. You can search the directory without registering or paying. Applications, identity checks and official payments take place on the destination portal.

Who maintains it

This site is maintained by Ansh Sharma. For questions, corrections or privacy requests, email anshansharma008@gmail.com. Digital Seva Hub is independent and is not a government department, an authorised application centre or a representative of the listed providers.

How we approach content

Guides explain preparation and safety steps in our own words. Official portals remain the source for current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements. We do not promise approval, benefits or faster processing. Provider names identify their services and do not imply endorsement.

Corrections and funding

If a link or explanation is inaccurate, send the page address and the official source that explains the correction. The directory is free. An optional payment section is separate from government services; read its terms before paying. This site currently has no advertising code. Any future advertising should be clearly identified and kept separate from service links.
