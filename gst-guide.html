GST registration and return preparation

Keep registration questions separate from ongoing filing tasks.

By Ansh Sharma · Published 9 November 2026

Independent preparation guide. Confirm current eligibility, fees, deadlines and document requirements with the authority. Reading this guide is free; applications take place on the official portal.
Before you startPreparation checklistNext stepsIf something goes wrong

Before you start

GST registration requirements depend on facts such as the nature of supplies, location and applicable rules. Do not decide from a single turnover figure found in an old article. Use current official guidance or qualified tax advice to establish what applies to your activity.

Your preparation checklist

  1. Identify whether your task is registration, amendment, return filing or payment.
  2. Prepare business and authorised-person records required for that specific task.
  3. For returns, reconcile relevant sales, purchases and supporting records first.
  4. Check the current form, period and applicable deadline on official sources.

Complete the next steps

Open the required GST service and verify the business identity and filing period. Review any draft or preview before submission, especially totals and declarations. Save the acknowledgement and any payment reference separately; paying a liability and successfully filing a return can involve different steps.

Open GST portal ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Official destination: https://www.gst.gov.in. The provider’s current instructions take precedence over this guide.

If something goes wrong

If records do not reconcile, investigate the difference before submitting rather than entering a balancing figure without evidence. A saved draft is not necessarily a filed return. Use official status tools and help channels for portal problems; seek professional advice for classification, liability or disputed tax treatment.
