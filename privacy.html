Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

Scope and contact

This policy covers Digital Seva Hub at freeseva.netlify.app, maintained by Ansh Sharma. Email anshansharma008@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information used by this website

The directory does not request accounts, government documents or application details. Search text and category selections are processed in your browser; the page does not save them or send them to a search service. The current site code does not set advertising cookies or use analytics trackers.

Hosting and fonts

Netlify hosts this site and may process technical request information such as IP addresses, browser details and requested URLs for delivery, security and operations. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives technical information, including your IP address, when your browser requests them. See Netlify’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy for their practices.

Email and payment enquiries

If you email support, your address, message and any transaction reference you supply are processed by our email provider and used to answer your enquiry, check payment receipt or address a dispute. The site itself has no payment-verification database. Your UPI app and payment providers handle the transfer under their own policies. We do not need your PIN, OTP or full financial credentials.

Retention and choices

Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve an enquiry or payment dispute and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by email; information needed for an unresolved dispute or legal obligation may need to be retained. Avoid sending sensitive documents. You can block third-party requests in your browser, although fonts may then appear differently.

Advertising and cookies

No Google AdSense ads or advertising scripts are currently installed. If Google advertising is introduced, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on visits to this and other websites. This policy and any required consent controls must be updated before those scripts are enabled. Learn about Google’s advertising data use at Google advertising technologies and manage personalisation at My Ad Center.

External websites and changes

Government portals, entertainment services and payment apps are separate services with their own privacy policies. Review them before submitting information. Material changes to this site’s data practices will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
