A few helpful answers.

Is Digital Seva Hub a government website?

No. This is an independent directory that helps you find official portals. We do not represent any government department or process applications.

Are government services free?

Our directory is free. Application, booking or other fees may apply on the official portals, according to their own rules.

How is my UPI payment confirmed?

Send the ₹49 payment reference (UTR) and date to anshansharma008@gmail.com. Support checks receipt manually and assists with access. This website cannot automatically verify a UPI transfer.

Does ₹49 include streaming subscriptions?

No. YouTube, JioHotstar, Spotify and other providers manage their own subscriptions. Public destination links here are free to browse.
